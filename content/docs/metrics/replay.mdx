title: Replay

Replay is an animated year-in-review built from your watch history. It's always accessible from the Replay tab in the header or the Replay button on the Metrics page. Use the account selector to view Replay for a specific account or combined across all accounts.

<Note>Replay is generated entirely from your locally stored watch history. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to share your Replay card.</Note>

## Sections

**Hero:** Opens with your year, total titles watched, total watch time, and your longest streak, all animated.

**Deep Insights:** Year-over-year comparison showing how your watching changed versus the previous year, including streaks, total plays, and watch time delta.

**Milestones:** Notable achievements from the year, such as completing a long series, hitting a watch streak record, or your biggest binge session.

**By Month:** A month-by-month breakdown of your activity showing peak months and quieter periods.

**Top Titles:** Your most-watched series and movies of the year with play counts.

**Year in Numbers:** A summary card covering total titles, episodes, movies, genres, and active accounts.

## Sharing

The Share button at the end of Replay generates a shareable link that anyone can view, even without an AIOManager account. The link encodes your Replay data and renders a read-only version of your year-in-review.

<Tip>Share links work without requiring the recipient to log in or have AIOManager set up.</Tip>
