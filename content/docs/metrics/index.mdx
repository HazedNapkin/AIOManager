---
title: Activity Feed
description: Track your watch history across all connected accounts locally and privately.
---

The Activity page shows your watch history pulled from Stremio across all connected accounts. Data is stored locally in your browser and never sent anywhere. Only items that appear in your Stremio "Continue Watching" list or Library with watch progress are included.

Use the account filter to view activity for a specific account or see everything combined. The **Refresh** button re-fetches the latest history from Stremio. If you've just watched something, wait 30 seconds for Stremio's API to update before refreshing.

<Note>If the activity feed is empty, Stremio's API may be slow to update. Wait 30 seconds and click Refresh. If you've never watched anything through Stremio with watch progress enabled, the feed will remain empty.</Note>

## Metrics Tabs

The Metrics page organises your stats into five tabs:

**Pulse:** Your real-time watching overview. Shows the Endurance Test (started vs engaged vs completed), The Loop (most replayed titles), Prime Time activity heatmap, Content Velocity, and the Streak Hall of Fame across all accounts.

**Community:** Cross-account comparisons when you have multiple Stremio identities. Includes Binge Mastery (most titles in one session), Community Awards (Midnight Snackers, Weekend Warriors, Completionists), and the Streak Hall of Fame ranked across all your accounts.

**Personality:** Behavioural analysis of your watching habits. Shows Watch Personas (role badges earned based on patterns) and Habit Profiles (chronotype, binge tendency, completion rate, and more).

**Vault:** Personal stats about what you've watched. Includes Genre DNA (series vs movies ratio), Time Dilation (total watch time), Series Loyalty score, and Activity Heatmap.

**Deep Dive:** Advanced analytics including the Retention Funnel (how far through series you actually get), The Graveyard (abandoned shows), and year-over-year comparison of your watching habits.
