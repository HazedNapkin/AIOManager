---
title: Metrics Tabs
description: A breakdown of every tab on the Metrics page and what each one shows.
---

The Metrics page organises your stats into five tabs. Each tab analyses a different dimension of your watching habits.

## Pulse

Your real-time watching overview. Shows:

- **Endurance Test:** how many titles you started vs engaged with vs completed
- **The Loop:** your most replayed titles
- **Prime Time:** an activity heatmap showing which hours and days you watch most
- **Content Velocity:** how quickly you're consuming content over time
- **Streak Hall of Fame:** longest streaks across all your accounts

## Community

Cross-account comparisons when you manage multiple Stremio identities. Includes:

- **Binge Mastery:** most titles watched in a single session per account
- **Community Awards:** Midnight Snackers, Weekend Warriors, Completionists badges
- **Streak Hall of Fame:** accounts ranked by their longest watch streak

## Personality

Behavioural analysis of your watching habits. Shows:

- **Watch Personas:** role badges earned based on your viewing patterns
- **Habit Profiles:** chronotype (when you watch), binge tendency, completion rate, and session length averages

## Vault

Personal stats about the content you've watched:

- **Genre DNA:** series vs movies ratio visualisation
- **Time Dilation:** total watch time expressed in human-readable format
- **Series Loyalty:** how consistently you finish series you start
- **Activity Heatmap:** your watching density over the calendar year

## Deep Dive

Advanced analytics for the data-curious:

- **Retention Funnel:** how far through series you actually get before stopping
- **The Graveyard:** shows you abandoned and never finished
- **Year-over-Year:** comparison of your watching habits across years
