## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step>**Choose Your Instance:** Use a [Public Instance](/kronorium/getting-started/public-instances) or [Self-Host](/kronorium/getting-started/installation) your own.</Step>
  <Step>**Login:** Open AIOManager in your browser and create your account with a strong password.</Step>
  <Step>**Add an Account:** Use the [First Account guide](/kronorium/getting-started/first-account) to connect a Stremio identity, create a local account, or link to a platform like Nuvio or RealStream.</Step>
  <Step>**Install Addons:** Browse, configure, and install addons through AIOManager. Check the **Discover** tab for community addons.</Step>
  <Step>**Set Up Connections:** Mirror your addon configuration to external platforms via the [Connections](/kronorium/accounts/connections) tab on any account.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>AIOManager requires HTTPS or localhost. Plain HTTP over a network IP will not work due to browser security restrictions on encryption APIs.</Note>
