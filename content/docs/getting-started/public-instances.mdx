Don't want to self-host? Community members provide public AIOManager instances you can use for free.

<Warning>Public instances are hosted by third parties. Your data (addons, settings, API keys) is stored on their servers. For maximum privacy and control, consider [self-hosting](/kronorium/getting-started/installation).</Warning>

## Before You Choose

<Tip>Each instance is completely independent: it has its own database, its own URL, and its own resource limits. Your configuration is stored only on the instance you use.</Tip>

**Considerations:**

- **Uptime:** instances may go offline without notice
- **Resource limits:** some hosts may cap the number of addons per user to ensure stability
- **Data retention:** check with the host about their data retention policies
- **Privacy:** your credentials and addon configurations are stored on someone else's server

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step>**Choose an Instance:** Select a tab above and click the URL.</Step>
  <Step>**Create an Account:** Set up your AIOManager login, which is separate from your Stremio account.</Step>
  <Step>**Save Your Credentials:** Store your UUID and password somewhere safe. There is no password reset.</Step>
  <Step>**Add Your Stremio Account:** Follow the [First Account guide](/kronorium/getting-started/first-account) to connect your Stremio identity.</Step>
</Steps>

## Self-Hosting

Want full control over your data and uptime? Check out the [Self-Hosting guide](/kronorium/getting-started/installation) to deploy your own instance with Docker, Unraid, or Node.js.
