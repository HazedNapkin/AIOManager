## General

**Display Name:** Your name shown in the header and Replay. Defaults to your UUID prefix.

**Privacy Mode:** Masks account emails and sensitive data across the UI. Persists across reloads. Useful for screenshots or screen sharing.

**Hide Disabled Addons:** Hides disabled addons from the installed list globally. A toggle appears in the addon toolbar when any addons are off. Syncs across devices.

**Global Webhook URL:** A Discord, Slack, or JSON webhook URL used by all Autopilot rules set to "Use global webhook". Changing it updates all rules at once.

**Notifications:** Toggle individual notification events on or off (Autopilot triggers, sync events, addon updates). Notifications are sent to the Global Webhook URL when enabled.

For theme settings see [Appearance & Themes](/kronorium/settings/appearance). For vault settings see [Key Vault](/kronorium/settings/vault).

## Data & Sync

**Push to Cloud:** Immediately uploads your current local state to the server.

**Pull from Cloud:** Downloads the latest state from the server and replaces your local data. Use when switching devices.

**Export Data:** Downloads a single `.json` file containing all your accounts, profiles, library, autopilot rules, themes, and settings. Keep this as a backup before any Danger Zone operation.

**Import Data:** Restores a previously exported `.json` file. Incoming data is merged with your current state; existing accounts and addons are preserved unless overwritten by the import.

**Clear Activity:** Deletes your local watch history cache. The next sync re-fetches it from your connected accounts.

<Note>Avoid using AIOManager on two devices simultaneously with the same sync ID. Both will push their state and the last write wins.</Note>

## Advanced & Danger Zone

The Advanced tab contains irreversible data operations. All actions require toggling **Unlock Actions** before any button becomes active.

**Wipe Accounts:** Removes all accounts from AIOManager. Does not delete anything from connected platforms.

**Wipe Library:** Deletes all saved addons from your Library.

**Purge Autopilot:** Deletes all Autopilot rules and their history from both local storage and the server.

**Factory Reset:** Wipes everything (accounts, library, autopilot, themes, settings) and reloads the app fresh. Your AIOManager login credentials are not affected.

<Tip>Always export a backup before using any Danger Zone action.</Tip>
