---
title: Advanced & Danger Zone
description: Irreversible data operations for resetting or wiping AIOManager data.
---

The Advanced tab contains irreversible data operations. All actions require toggling **Unlock Actions** before any button becomes active.

<Warning>These actions cannot be undone. Export a backup from Settings → Data & Sync before proceeding.</Warning>

## Actions

**Wipe Accounts:** Removes all accounts from AIOManager. Does not delete anything from connected platforms (Stremio, Nuvio, RealStream, etc.); those accounts and their addons are untouched.

**Wipe Library:** Deletes all saved addons from your Library.

**Purge Autopilot:** Deletes all Autopilot rules and their history from both local storage and the server.

**Factory Reset:** Wipes everything (accounts, library, autopilot, themes, settings) and reloads the app fresh. Your AIOManager login credentials (UUID + password) are not affected.

<Tip>Always export a backup before using any Danger Zone action.</Tip>
