title: Advanced & Danger Zone
description: Irreversible data operations for resetting or wiping AIOManager data.
---

The Advanced tab holds maintenance tools, remembered sign-in management, and irreversible data operations. Danger Zone actions additionally require toggling **Unlock Actions** before any button becomes active.

## Remembered Devices

Every browser where you ticked **Remember this device** at sign-in is listed here, with its label (the browser and device it identified itself as), unlock tier (**Passkey** or **Device key**), the date it was saved, and when it was last used. Your current browser is marked **This device**.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Revoke** | That browser's saved sign-in stops working immediately. Nothing is deleted from your data; the browser simply asks for your UUID and password next time. |
| **Sign out everywhere** | All remembered devices are revoked at once. Use it after losing a device, or whenever you want a clean slate. |

<Note>The list shows remembered browsers only. Regular password sign-ins are not tracked.</Note>

See [Remember This Device](/kronorium/getting-started/remember-this-device) for how saved sign-ins work.

## Danger Zone

<Warning>These actions cannot be undone. Export a backup from Settings → Data & Sync before proceeding.</Warning>

## Actions

**Wipe Accounts:** Removes all accounts from AIOManager. Does not delete anything from connected platforms (Stremio, Nuvio, RealStream, etc.); those accounts and their addons are untouched.

**Wipe Library:** Deletes all saved addons from your Library.

**Purge Autopilot:** Deletes all Autopilot rules and their history from both local storage and the server.

**Factory Reset:** Wipes everything (accounts, library, autopilot, themes, settings) and reloads the app fresh. Your AIOManager login credentials (UUID + password) are not affected.

<Tip>Always export a backup before using any Danger Zone action.</Tip>
